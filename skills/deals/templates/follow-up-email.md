# Post-call follow-up email template

Body hard limit: 170 words. Write in the user's voice (`context/voice.md`). Every claim beyond what the buyer said must trace to `context/evidence.md` or `context/offers.md`; otherwise `[NEEDS PROOF: <what>]`.

```markdown
DRAFT · not sent
To: <buyer email or name> · Cc: <only people on the call or named by the buyer>
Subject: <their words for the problem> · next step <Day DD Mon>

Hi <first name>,

Thanks for the time today. What I heard:
- Right now: <current state, their words, quoted where short> 
- Where you want to be: <future state, their words>
- What it's costing: <impact in their numbers, e.g. "around 60 missed appointments a week"> 

My recommendation: <ONE option, named exactly as in offers.md> because <one reason tied to the impact above>. <If they weighed several options: "I'd leave <other> until <condition>; it doesn't touch <their stated problem>.">

Next step: <buyer action> with <who> on <Day DD Mon, time>. <If not yet agreed: "Does <Day DD Mon> or <Day DD Mon> work better?">

<Optional, one line: what they will have before that date, only if promised on the call.>

<Sign-off from voice.md>
```

## Variant rules

| Call outcome (from scorecard) | Next-step paragraph |
|---|---|
| Advance agreed with date | Confirm it exactly as agreed: action, who, date. No new asks. |
| Advance agreed, no date | Propose two specific dates within 5 business days. |
| Continuation ("send me some info") | Send only the one item they asked for, framed as input to a specific Advance: "So it's useful, I'd suggest we go through it together with <who> on <date> or <date>; 20 minutes." Never a plain "here's the info, let me know". |
| Stall: valuation | Recommendation paragraph is mandatory and names exactly one option. |
| Stall: information | Name the 2-3 criteria that decide it and where you stand on each; say more research will not change the picture; propose a decision date. |
| Stall: outcome | Add one risk-reduction term that exists in offers.md (pilot, phase, support, exit), or `[NEEDS APPROVAL: <term>]` for the user to decide. |
| Stall: status-quo | No recommendation yet. Ask one Implication question in their words and propose a short call to size it. |

## Never
- "Just checking in", "circling back", "touching base", "as promised, attached is our deck".
- Deadlines, discounts or scarcity that are not real and written in `context/pricing.md`; never any of them when the stall is indecision.
- Quotes the buyer did not say, or numbers rounded up from what they said.
- More than one recommendation, or more than one call to action.
