# Prep sheet template (one page)

Hard limit: 650 words for everything above "Research notes". Research notes may run longer but every line carries a source. Second person ("you") is fine here: the sheet is read by the user before the call.

```markdown
# Prep · <Company> · <Person, role> · <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM tz> · <call type: discovery | follow-up | proposal review>
Deal file: deals/<slug>.md (<new | stage, last touch YYYY-MM-DD>) · Source: <outreach | referral | inbound | …>

## In one line
<Who they are, what they likely struggle with, and the Advance you want. ≤ 30 words.>

## Them
- Business: <what they sell, to whom, size signals> [source]
- Person: <role; what someone in this role is measured on; 1 public work signal if any> [source]
- Likely priorities: 1. <…> 2. <…> 3. <…> (inferred from role + research; confirm on the call)

## Job to be done and forces (Moesta; Christensen)
- Job: "When <situation>, they want to <progress>, so they can <outcome>." (hypothesis)
- Push (struggle now): <…> · Pull (better day): <…>
- Anxiety (fear of switching): <…> · Habit (current way): <…>
- Timeline stage guess: <first thought | passive | active looking | deciding> → sell to that stage: <one line>

## Gap hypotheses to test (Keenan)

## Question tree (SPIN · Rackham)
Opening (purpose, not credentials): "<one line>"
Situation (only what research could not answer, max 3): S1 … S3
Problem: P1 [H1] … P2 [H1] … P3 [H2] … P4 [H3] …
Implication: I1 [H1] … I2 [H1] … I3 [H2] … I4 [H3] …
Need-payoff: N1 [H1] … N2 [H2] …
If they open with a solution (Khalsa): "Happy to get into that. So I build the right thing, what would <their solution> solve for you?"
Decision and resources (Khalsa ORDER): "Who else says yes on something like this, and what will they want to see?"
Summary to aim for "that's right" (Voss): "So today <current, their words>, you want <future>, and the gap is costing <impact>. Did I get that right?"

## Trust moves (Maister: lower self-orientation)

## Advance (Rackham)
- Target: <buyer action> · <who> · <by when> · Ask: "<exact words>"
- Fallback: <smaller buyer action> · <who> · <by when> · Ask: "<exact words>"
- Not acceptable: "send me some info", "let's keep in touch", "I'll think about it" without a date.
- Decisiveness ping (JOLT Judge): "<one question that reveals how they make decisions>"
```
