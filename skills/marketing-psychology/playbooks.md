# Playbooks

Each playbook chains several books into a real task. Book numbers in brackets.

## 1. Homepage and landing-page message
**Goal**: a page a business owner understands in 10 seconds and trusts in 60.
**Inputs**: the one outcome you deliver; 2–3 client results with names and numbers; the owner's implicit goal (03); your distinctive assets (10).
**Steps**
1. Write the Commander's Intent in one sentence; everything else serves it (05 Simple).
2. Diagnose the implicit goal of your typical buyer (security/autonomy for operators) and strip any signal that primes excitement/complexity (03, 04 priming).
3. Hero: Unexpected hook about the *reader's* process, then the core, filmable (05 Unexpected, Concrete; 04 focal problem).
4. Proof block: one Sinatra credential, one human-scale statistic, one named Challenge story with you as the hinge (05 Credible, Stories; 06 valuable virality).
5. Barrier block: answer the five REDUCE barriers in order: menu of ways to start, cost of inaction, smallest first step, reversibility, three similar proofs (07).
6. Price shown once, monthly, beside the cost of the problem; premium tier first (02, 01 contrast, 03 frame).
7. Trust line: one real limitation stated plainly (01, 09).
8. Distinctive assets applied: one colour, one diagram style, Fakhar's face; booking link visible at every scroll (10).
9. Ban-list pass: remove semantic stretch (05). Film test on every claim.
**Output artifact**: hero (hook, core, CTA) · proof block (3 items) · "how to start" menu (3 options) · reversibility line · price with reference point · limitation line · assets checklist.
**Common failure**: leading with the stack or the founder story (curse of knowledge, 05; misdirected attention, 04).

## 2. Discovery call: from opener to proposal-ready
**Goal**: leave the call with the problem quantified in the owner's words, the barrier diagnosed, and a yes to a scoped next step.
**Inputs**: trigger event that prompted the call (02 habit); the referrer or proof you can borrow (09 association).
**Steps**
1. Pre-call prime: invite title names their problem; booking page signals calm and competence (04, 03).
2. Minute 0–3: focal question on the hand-off that hurts, then "and that's how many hours a week?" (04 attention; 05 human-scale).
3. Self-description opener: "the kind of owner who fixes it once?" (04, 01 commitment).
4. Trust early: one thing you won't do for them (01 authority via admission, 09 trustworthiness).
5. Let them state the cost of inaction in £; restate it back (07 Endowment; 07 ask-don't-tell).
6. Diagnose the live barrier from their language (cheatsheet tree) and apply the matching REDUCE move (07).
7. Messenger switch: decisive if they're anxious, collaborative if exploring (09).
8. Pre-ask then menu: "If I mapped this and showed you which workflow first, would you want it?" → audit / pilot / retainer (04, 07 menu, 01 contrast).
9. Close with advice-asking: "what would you tell us to avoid?" (01 unity).
**Output artifact**: call sheet with: trigger · problem in their words · £/month cost of inaction · implicit goal · barrier diagnosed · chosen option · their advice · proof to send (3 similar).
**Common failure**: presenting the roadmap (07 Distance) or answering an uncertainty objection with more features (03 pilot overload).

## 3. Proposal and pricing
**Goal**: a proposal that is approved without a price negotiation.
**Inputs**: call sheet from Playbook 2; three comparable client results; your capacity (01 real scarcity).
**Steps**
1. Page 1 opens with their number: the £/month cost of inaction in their words (02 primacy, 07 Endowment).
2. State the implicit goal and the outcome in one filmable sentence (03, 05).
3. Three tiers, premium first; each described by outcome, each a single monthly figure (01 contrast, 02 pain of payment).
4. Reference point on the same page: the salaried alternative or the problem cost (02 price relativity).
5. Costly signals instead of discounts: setup-fee refund if not live in 30 days, named engineer, weekly check-in (08, 09).
6. Reversibility clause: old process left intact, cancel monthly (07 Uncertainty).
7. Three similar short proofs plus an offer to speak to one client (07 Corroborating Evidence, 01 similar social proof).
8. One stated limitation (01, 02 pratfall).
9. Real capacity line: "two implementations a month; next slot opens [date]" (01 scarcity, only if true).
10. Name the programme (08).
**Output artifact**: proposal skeleton: their number → outcome sentence → 3 tiers (premium first) → reference point → guarantees → reversibility → 3 proofs → limitation → capacity → named programme.
**Common failure**: itemised hours; defending the price instead of re-anchoring it (03, 02).

## 4. LinkedIn content system (mental availability engine)
**Goal**: be the name that comes to mind when an owner's process breaks.
**Inputs**: 6–10 buying situations (10 category entry points); your fixed distinctive assets (10); client stories cleared for public use (06 Public).
**Steps**
1. List the buying situations ("new ops hire asks where the process is", "Friday invoice chasing", "CRM migration", "owner wants to step back"); each is a recurring theme (10, 06 Triggers).
2. Post 5×/week to the whole segment, not just ideal firms (10 reach, light buyers).
3. Every post: one unfinished/counterintuitive hook (04, 05 Unexpected), one filmable scene (05), one social-currency line (06), one trigger reference ("it's the 28th...") (06).
4. Emotion: open with absurdity or relief, not calm (06).
5. Weekly practical-value piece narrowed to one segment, savings in pounds (06 Practical Value, Rule of 100).
6. Monthly Challenge story where the turning point is your workflow (05, 06 Trojan Horse); tag the client (06 Public).
7. Quarterly trust post: "cases where we said don't hire us" (09, 01).
8. Vulnerability post only after a run of results (09, 02 pratfall).
9. Assets unchanged for 24 months; measure profile views and inbound by situation, not likes (10).
10. A/B hooks weekly; keep what books calls (02).
**Output artifact**: theme calendar (situations × weekdays) · post template (hook / scene / line / trigger / CTA) · asset lock sheet · metrics sheet.
**Common failure**: posting about "AI" rather than about the owner's situations (10); rebranding mid-year (10); generic tips for everyone (06).

## 5. Objection and hesitation handling
**Goal**: convert "not now / not sure / too much" into a scoped first step.
**Inputs**: the objection verbatim; what's changing in their business (02 habit, 07 burn the ships).
**Steps**
1. Restate their position fully before answering (07 Reactance, 04 understanding first).
2. Ask the diagnostic: "timing, size of change, or not sure it'll work here?" (07).
3. Reactance → menu of 2–3; Endowment → cost of inaction in £; Distance → one workflow, switch field to their goal; Uncertainty → pilot, fixed fee, reversible; Evidence → three similar proofs within 48 hours (07).
4. Recommend against yourself where true: "if volume is under X, don't" (09, 01).
5. Hold price; add a costly signal if price is raised (08).
6. Pre-ask then small commitment: "would it help if I...?" (04, 01).
7. Real scarcity last, never first (01, 07).
**Output artifact**: objection → barrier → move → script line, one row per objection.
**Common failure**: more arguments (raises reactance); discounting (signals doubt, 03).

## 6. Offer design and naming for a new service
**Goal**: a new AI-automation offer owners can picture, trust and start.
**Inputs**: the human step it replaces; the implicit goal it serves (03); comparable offers' prices.
**Steps**
1. Doorman check: list every hidden function of the human step and decide how each is preserved (08).
2. Net value sheet: all rewards (tied to the implicit goal) and all pains (price, effort, risk, time, lock-in); remove pains before pricing (03).
3. Shrink the entry: audit → pilot → retainer ladder, each reversible (07 Distance, Uncertainty).
4. Price with a reference point and a costly signal, not a discount (02, 08).
5. Name it concretely; make the name a trigger ("the Quiet Back Office", "the Hand-off Audit") (08, 05, 06).
6. Write the Sinatra credential and the first Challenge story you'll earn with it (05).
7. Test two names and two price frames on 50 prospects each before launch (02).
**Output artifact**: offer one-pager: name · goal served · hidden functions preserved · pains removed · ladder · price + reference · signal · credential.
**Common failure**: designing for the average buyer (08); selling adventure to a security buyer (03).
