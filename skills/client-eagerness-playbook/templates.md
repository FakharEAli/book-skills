## §R Proposal review checklist (short playbook)
- [ ] One outcome, one price, one guarantee; no menu (03)
- [ ] One scoped "no"; one recommendation that costs us something; zero urgency lines (05)
- [ ] Every claim tagged with evidence or removed (06)
- [ ] Week-one plan, sample output on their data, "what we will not do" (07)
- [ ] Recommendation, not options; next small step named; their stated fear answered in their words (09, 08)
