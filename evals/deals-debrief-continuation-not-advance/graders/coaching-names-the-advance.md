Generic advice ("ask for next steps") is what any answer gives. The plugin's coaching must name a specific Advance that fits what Gareth said: Leanne runs maintenance, "Leanne would know better" on volumes, and "Leanne would need to be on board".

**PASS** if the coaching (summary or coaching notes) names at least one concrete Advance that:
- Is an action by the buyer side (Gareth and/or Leanne), not only by Jo, e.g. a 30-minute session with Leanne to walk through how maintenance comes in; Leanne or Gareth pulling a month's maintenance message count or a sample week from WhatsApp/Fixflo; a call with Gareth and Leanne to size the problem, and

- Is given as an exact line Jo could have said at the close (e.g. "Rather than send something generic, could we get 30 minutes with Leanne on Thursday so I can see how jobs actually come in?").

- The coaching only says "ask for next steps", "book a follow-up" or "get a commitment" with no specific action, person or time.

- No coaching note addresses the close of the call.
