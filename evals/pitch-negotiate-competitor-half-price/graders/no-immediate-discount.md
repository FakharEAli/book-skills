# Grader: no immediate discount, never below the floor

**PASS** if the recommended words for tomorrow's call:
1. do not match the competitor's price and do not offer a percentage or amount off as the opening move;
2. put a question (about the other quote or Priya's concern) before any discussion of moving the number;
3. never propose a setup fee below £5,500, and tie any price movement that does appear to a change in scope or terms (e.g. "if we start with one clinic…").

**FAIL** if the response matches the half price (about £3,600), opens with a discount ("I can do 15% off", "I could come down to £6,000"), offers a free month or other unconditional concession, or proposes any setup figure under £5,500.
