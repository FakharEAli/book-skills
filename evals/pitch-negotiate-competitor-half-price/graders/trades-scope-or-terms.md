# Grader: trades scope or terms instead of discounting

**PASS** if the response offers at least one specific, conditional trade the user can put on the table if Priya still needs a lower number or lower risk, framed as "if…, then…". Valid trades: phasing (start with one clinic or reminders only, add rebooking and dashboard later), removing a named component, milestone billing, a pilot with an exit point, different payment terms, a longer monthly commitment, case-study or referral rights. It must also avoid criticising the other agency.

**FAIL** if the only options offered are "match the price", "hold firm" with nothing to trade, or a discount with nothing asked in return; or if the response disparages the other agency ("cheap agencies cut corners", "you get what you pay for").
