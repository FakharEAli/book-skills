# Grader: moves off the solution with a question

**PASS** if the drafted reply acknowledges that Zapier is in place without contesting it, and then asks one open question that gets underneath it to the business problem: what Zapier is not handling today, which steps are still done by hand, or where quote requests still wait (e.g. "What's the part Zapier isn't covering yet, the bits someone still does by hand?"). This is Khalsa's "move off the solution".

**FAIL** if the reply has no such question; argues that the sender's service is better than Zapier; lists features to compare; asks only a closed yes/no question unrelated to what Zapier does or doesn't do (e.g. "Are you happy with Zapier?"); or stacks two or more questions before the next-step ask.
