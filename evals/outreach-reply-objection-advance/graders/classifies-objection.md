# Grader: classifies the reply as an objection and diagnoses it

**PASS** if the response explicitly classifies Sam's reply as an **objection** (interest plus a reason not to proceed), not as "not interested", "unsubscribe" or "interested", AND names what is behind it: the status quo feels adequate / "why change?" (Hoffeld's Six Whys), or that the buyer has anchored on an existing tool or solution. A brief mention of either diagnosis is enough.

**FAIL** if the response gives no classification, treats the reply as a rejection to be accepted or dropped, treats it as a buying signal and jumps straight to pricing or a pitch, or responds to the surface words (a Zapier feature comparison) without identifying the underlying status-quo or existing-solution issue.
