# Grader: proposes a concrete Advance with a time

Rackham (SPIN Selling) separates an Advance, an agreed buyer action that moves the sale forward, from a Continuation, a friendly exchange with no agreed action.

**PASS** if the drafted reply proposes one specific next step with a specific day, date or time (e.g. "a 20-minute walkthrough Thursday at 10:00 or Friday at 14:00", "send me the two steps your team still does by hand and I'll come back with a one-page outline by Wednesday"). Two offered time slots count. Placeholders like "[CHECK CALENDAR]" next to specific proposed slots count.

**FAIL** if the next step has no time or date ("let me know if you'd like to chat", "happy to share more sometime", "would you be open to a call?"), if no next step is proposed, or if the reply only sends information with no buyer action.
