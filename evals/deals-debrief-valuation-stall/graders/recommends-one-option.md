# Grader: the follow-up recommends exactly one package

Priya asked for a recommendation three times and Sam deflected. Her stated pain is cancelled slots sitting empty (waitlist "on paper", "nobody has time to ring round") and forgetting (one email reminder). She said phones are fine, intake via Cliniko forms "work fine", and the dashboard is "nice to have". The package whose scope matches her stated problem with the least extra scope is **Fill**.

**PASS** if the follow-up email draft:
- Recommends exactly one package by name, stated as a recommendation ("I'd start with Fill", "my recommendation is Fill"), and
- Gives a reason tied to what Priya said (empty slots, the paper waitlist, the sixty slots, or the physios' empty days), and
- Does not present the three packages as equal choices. Mentioning another package once as "later, if…" or "not needed because…" is fine.

Recommending Remind with a reason tied to her words is a weaker answer but still PASSES only if it explicitly addresses why backfill is not needed; Fill is the expected answer.

**FAIL** if any of:
- The email recommends Front Desk (she said the phones are fine and intake works).
- The email recommends two packages, offers a choice ("Fill or Front Desk, whichever suits"), or says "it depends".
- The email attaches or lays out a side-by-side comparison of all three as the main content.
- No email draft is produced.
