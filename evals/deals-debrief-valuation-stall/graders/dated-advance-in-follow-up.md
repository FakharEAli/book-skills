# Grader: the follow-up proposes a dated Advance

The call ended with "let me have a think… I'll come back to you", a Continuation (Rackham). Martin Hale signs off anything over five grand and will go with Priya's recommendation. A good follow-up proposes a specific buyer action with a date, ideally involving Martin or the sign-off.

**PASS** if the follow-up email draft:
- Proposes a specific next step that requires an action from Priya (or Priya and Martin), e.g. a 15-20 minute call to confirm Fill, a session to put the recommendation to Martin, or a decision call, and
- Attaches a concrete date or day after 2026-10-06 (e.g. "Thursday 9 October" or two specific options), and
- Has a single call to action.
- In any deal-file update, the next step is marked as proposed (e.g. "PROPOSED:") or otherwise not recorded as agreed.

**FAIL** if any of:
- The email ends with "let me know", "any questions, shout", "no rush", or "take your time" with no dated step.
- The next step is only something Sam does (send the comparison) with no buyer action.
- The date proposed is on or before 2026-10-06, or the deal file records the date as agreed by Priya.
- The email uses invented urgency (deadlines, limited slots, price rises).
