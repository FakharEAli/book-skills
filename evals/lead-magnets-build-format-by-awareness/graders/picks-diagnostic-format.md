# Grader: picks a diagnostic format, not the ROI calculator

The fixture describes owners who do not believe they have a missed-call problem (Schwartz: Unaware). The correct format is a diagnostic scorecard / self-assessment.

**PASS** if all of these hold:
- The response's primary recommendation and the magnet it actually builds is a diagnostic scorecard, self-assessment, quiz or audit-style questionnaire that lets the owner discover the problem.
- It explicitly declines or defers the user's ROI-calculator idea as the primary magnet (it may mention it as a later-stage asset or alternative).

**FAIL** if any of these hold:
- It builds an ROI calculator, revenue-recovery calculator, cost calculator or "hidden cost" report as the primary magnet.
- It builds two or more magnets with equal weight and does not pick one.
- It agrees with the ROI calculator without addressing its fit with the segment.
