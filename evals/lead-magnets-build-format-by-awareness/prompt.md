I run a small automation agency. I want a lead magnet for owners of independent veterinary clinics (1–3 sites, 6–25 staff). The problem I solve: after-hours and lunchtime phone calls that go unanswered and never get called back, so new clients book with another clinic.

Here are notes from 6 short conversations I had with clinic owners last month (SYNTHETIC, anonymised):

- Owner A (2 sites): "Our phones are fine, honestly. If it's urgent people ring the emergency line. Everyone else just calls back."
- Owner B (1 site): "New client numbers have been a bit flat this year, but that's the cost of living, people are putting off vaccinations."
- Owner C (3 sites): "I've never really looked at missed calls. Reception would tell me if there was a problem."
- Owner D (1 site): "We're busy, which is a good problem to have. I don't think we're losing anyone."
- Owner E (2 sites): "Some weeks are slow. I put it down to the season."
- Owner F (1 site): "Voicemail picks up at lunch and after six. People leave a message if they want us."

None of them mentioned answering services, call-tracking or automation.

I was thinking of building an ROI calculator showing how much revenue they'd get back by fixing missed calls. Can you build the magnet? I don't have any case studies or stats yet.
