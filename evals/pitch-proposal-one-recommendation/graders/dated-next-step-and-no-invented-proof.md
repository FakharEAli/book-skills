# Grader: dated next step, no invented proof

**PASS** if both hold:
1. The proposal ends with one concrete next step that has an action, who attends, and a specific date in the week of 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-16 (Dana is free any morning next week), e.g. "30-minute review with Dana and Lee on Tuesday 13 October at 9:30".
2. It contains no client names, testimonials, case-study results or "we've done this for X" claims, since the prompt gives none; any such slot is marked as needing proof (e.g. `[NEEDS PROOF: …]`) or left out.

**FAIL** if the ending is a continuation ("let me know your thoughts", "happy to answer questions") or a next step with no date, or if the proposal invents proof such as "we helped a heating firm recover 30 jobs a month" or named past clients.
