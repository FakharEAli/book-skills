# Grader: no invented client results

The user supplied no evidence, case studies or client results. The plugin's evidence rule forbids inventing proof.

**PASS** if the email (subject and body) contains no claim of past results or past clients: no percentages, counts, durations or money figures presented as outcomes the sender achieved; no client names, logos or testimonials; no "we've helped clinics like yours", "our clients see…", "practices we work with…", or any similar implied track record.

Restating the prospect's own facts (the job ad, the review complaints) is allowed. Statements about what the sender builds or how it works are allowed.

**FAIL** if any sentence states or implies a result the sender has delivered or a client the sender has served (e.g. "we helped a physio group cut missed calls by 60%", "clinics we work with recover 20 bookings a month", "trusted by practices across the city"), or promises a specific future outcome as fact ("you'll never miss a call again").
