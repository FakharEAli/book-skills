# Grader: length limits, subject format, question CTA

**PASS** if all hold:
1. Email body is 90 words or fewer (count from the greeting through the last sentence before the sign-off; a `[NEEDS PROOF: …]` marker counts as one word).
2. Subject line is 5 words or fewer and entirely lower-case.
3. The body's final sentence before the sign-off is a question ending in "?", and it is a low-friction question (e.g. "Worth a look?", "Is this on your radar?", a no-oriented question), not a request for a call or meeting slot.
4. The email opens on Fernbrook's own trigger (the front-desk job ad or the "nobody called back" reviews), not on the sender.

**FAIL** if the body exceeds 90 words, the subject has more than 5 words or any capital letter, the email ends on a statement or sign-off line with no question, the CTA asks for a meeting or call ("15 minutes this week?", "when can we hop on a call?"), or it contains "I hope this finds you well", "quick question" or "AI-powered".
