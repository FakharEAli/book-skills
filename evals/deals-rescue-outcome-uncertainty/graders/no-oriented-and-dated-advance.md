# Grader: includes a no-oriented variant, a dated Advance and an alternative channel

The plugin's rescue output has three parts that a generic answer usually lacks: a Voss no-oriented question variant, a small dated buyer action, and a second channel.

**PASS** if the response includes all of:
- A no-oriented question in a message draft, in the style of "Have you given up on <getting every quote followed up / fixing the quote chasing>?" or "Would it be a bad idea to…?", using Dana's problem in her terms.
- A small, specific next step for Dana (and ideally Kerry) with a concrete day or date after 2026-10-06, e.g. 20 minutes to walk Kerry through what happens if it stops, or a decision on Phase 1 only.
- An alternative-channel version (phone/voicemail script, text, or a note via Kerry as champion), short and consistent with the same lever.

**FAIL** if any of:
- No no-oriented question appears anywhere.
- The primary message ends without a dated buyer action (e.g. "let me know if you have questions", "whenever suits").
- No alternative channel is offered.
- The response claims a message was sent rather than presenting drafts.
